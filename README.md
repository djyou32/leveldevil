## What Is Level Devil?

Level Devil is a side-scrolling platformer built around one simple idea: **easy to control, hard to survive**. Your goal in every room is to walk the little character to the exit door. The catch is that the level itself is playing tricks on you — the moment you trust the floor, it collapses; the moment you jump, spikes pop out of nowhere.

Typical Level Devil gameplay looks like this:

- **Hidden spikes** – a patch of ground that seems empty suddenly grows spikes as you step or jump on it.
- **Collapsing floors** – platforms vanish beneath your feet, sometimes the instant you land on them.
- **Falling ceilings** – blocks drop from above only after you walk into their trigger zone.
- **Moving walls and doors** – walls shift to block your path, and even the exit door can move away at the last second.

Because each room is small, failure costs you only a few seconds. Every death teaches you exactly where the trap was hiding, so progress comes from memorizing, adjusting your timing, and retrying — one careful move at a time.

The [English homepage](https://level-devil.org/en/) hosts the original Level Devil game. **Keyboard controls:**

- Move left: `A` or `←`
- Move right: `D` or `→`

On mobile, on-screen buttons appear beside the game: the left-side buttons move your character left and right, and the right-side button jumps. If the controls shown inside the game differ, follow the key instructions displayed in the game itself.

### Two-Player Mode

Yes — Level Devil can be played by two people at the same computer. The [two-player guide](https://level-devil.org/en/two-player) explains the local co-op setup on one PC and one keyboard:

- **Player 1** moves with `W`, `A` and `D`
- **Player 2** moves with the arrow keys

Note that this is local multiplayer only — both players share the same keyboard, not an online matchmaking session.

## Quick FAQ

**Is Level Devil free?**
Yes. The browser version on the site is completely free, with no download or installation required.

**Does Level Devil work on mobile?**
Yes. On mobile, use the on-screen buttons — left and right on the left side of the screen, jump on the right.

**Why is Level Devil so hard?**
The controls are simple, but floors disappear, spikes appear without warning, walls move, and even the exit can relocate. Every failure reveals how a trap works — that is how you get better.

**Can two people play together?**
Yes, in a local two-player mode on one PC and one keyboard. It is not online multiplayer.
